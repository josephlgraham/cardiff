Every plant and animal people have recorded on iNaturalist in Graysville, Cardiff, and Brookside, with the first time each one turned up and the most recent. A species that goes on this list stays on it, however long it goes before anybody sees another one.

Where this comes from Records from iNaturalist, research grade only, which means other people looked at each one and agreed on the identification. Only records from Graysville, Cardiff, and Brookside are kept, and no place closer than the town is ever written down. It comes from the same file as the sightings on Nature Watch, so the two cannot disagree. Where a species is in the field guide, its row links to the entry.
