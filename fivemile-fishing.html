Why water temperature runs the whole thing

Fish are cold blooded, so the temperature of the water sets how fast they digest, how much they need to eat, and how far they will move to get it. That is why the same creek can feel empty in February and busy in June. The figure in the tile above is an estimate built from air temperature and the time of year, not a thermometer in the water.

Before you eat what you catch

Alabama publishes a fish consumption advisory every year

The state updates advisories annually, waterbody by waterbody and species by species, from sampling done the previous fall. Five Mile Creek runs through a long industrial history, so this is not a formality here. Check the current advisory for this creek before you eat anything out of it.

Source: Alabama Department of Public Health, Fish Advisories, updated annually from Alabama Department of Environmental Management sampling.
