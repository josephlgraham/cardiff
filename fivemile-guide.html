Before you go out

Snakes

Most of the snakes you meet here are not the one you are worried about

Along moving water the northern water snake is far commoner than the cottonmouth, and it gets killed for the resemblance every summer. Neither one wants the encounter. Give it room, walk around it, and do not put a hand anywhere you have not looked. Long pants, boots, and gaiters from March to October cover the part of you a snake can actually reach.

Ticks and chiggers

Light clothes, tucked cuffs, and a check when you get home

Light colored clothing lets you see a tick before it reaches skin. Tuck your pants into your socks in tall grass, which is where the chiggers are. Check yourself thoroughly afterward, and check children and dogs too.

Fire

Wildfire season runs February through May, then again in the fall

We live in the woods. Clear underbrush around your property when and where you can, do not burn on a windy day, and keep a defensible space. Fire connects your land to your neighbor's, so what you clear protects both. The state issues burn permits and drought notices.
