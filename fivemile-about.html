About the site

FIVEMILE tells you what is going on in Graysville, Cardiff, and Brookside right now. The weather. The creek. When the council meets. What is happening this weekend. That is the whole idea, and it is why the address is fivemile.now.

What this is for

This is one place. One creek, the same hills, the same weather over all of it, and a good many of the same families. We cover it that way.

  • 📣Put it where people can find it. What the weather and the creek are doing before you go out in them, where the greenway runs, and what is being planned for here. We put it up plainly and leave the deciding to you.
  • 🕰️Keep it afterward. A year from now somebody wants to know what the creek did last March, or what used to stand on a lot. The Facebook groups are good for talking and no use for remembering. This is the other half.

Send us something

Photographs, announcements, and events are welcome from anybody in any of the three towns. Write to fivemilec@gmail.com.

By sending us a photograph you are giving us permission to publish it on this site with your name, unless you tell us not to.

Before you send

  • 🤝Be neighborly. Treat everyone the way you would if you ran into them at the mailbox. Disagreements are fine. Disrespect is not.
  • 🚫No hate speech, bigotry, or discrimination. Anything that targets a person for their race, ethnicity, religion, gender, or identity will not be approved. Period.
  • ✍️You are responsible for what you send. Everything is reviewed before it goes up, and anything published here is published because a person decided to publish it.

If we get something wrong

Tell us and we will fix it. Write to fivemilec@gmail.com. Somebody reading this almost certainly knows more about some corner of these three towns than the site does, and that is the point.

How this site is made

  • 🚫There is no AI running on this site. Nothing on these pages is written or decided by a machine while you read it. When the creek page says the water is up, that number came off a gauge. There is no chatbot here and there is not going to be one.
  • ⚙️Automatic is not the same thing as AI. The news page gathers headlines twice a day and sorts them by rules a person wrote down and can read back. No model decides what goes where, and every headline links straight to whoever reported it. It is the one page where something reaches you before I have seen it.
  • 🛠️I do use AI tools to build the site. Claude helps me write code, draft pages, and catch things I miss. It works for me the way a good tool does, and it does not decide anything.
  • ✍️A person decides what runs. Everything written in FIVEMILE's own voice is written or edited by me before it goes up. Nothing publishes on its own.
  • 📷No invented photographs, ever. Nothing here is an AI generated image and nothing ever will be. Historical photographs are real, sourced, and credited. Around here people can recognize their own grandparents in a picture, and a fake one would be unforgivable.
  • 📖No invented quotes, people, or history. When something is not known, the site says it is not known.
  • 🙋The mistakes are still mine. Using a tool is not an excuse for getting something wrong. Tell me and I will fix it, and say that I fixed it.

Support this project

This is an independent community website built on personal time and personal money. Contributions support the development, upkeep, hosting, tools, and expansion of the site itself. They do not go to the City of Graysville, the Town of Cardiff, the Town of Brookside, or to any official municipal account.

I am building this because these three towns are home, and I want the place to have a living record that is useful, welcoming, and worth returning to. Support helps me keep documenting it, improving the site, and adding the tools this project still needs.
