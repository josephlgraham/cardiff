Where these come from

Headlines here are gathered twice a day, morning and evening, from the outlets that cover this part of Jefferson County. We run the headline, one sentence, and the outlet's name, and every one of them links straight to the original. We do not republish anybody's article.

Weather alerts, conditions, and the creek gauge do not wait for an edition. Those are checked every ten minutes, day and night, so the red strip and the morning report are current even when the headlines are hours old.

When a photograph appears beside a headline, it is the picture the outlet itself published for sharing, loaded from that outlet's own server and credited to them. We never copy a photograph onto this site and we never take one out of the body of an article. If you publish here and you would rather we did not show your pictures at all, say so and we will turn them off for your site the same day.
