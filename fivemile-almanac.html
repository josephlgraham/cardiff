Sun and moon, what the weather did yesterday, where the creek stands this morning, and the week in front of you. Four more desks run off the same numbers.

  • 🎣Fishing. Water temperature, oxygen, and when the moon says the fish should feed.

Where this comes from Stage and flow are read from the US Geological Survey gauge 02457595, Fivemile Creek near Republic, which is the only live gauge left in this watershed. Rain totals come from the FIVEMILE weather station. The gauge and the station are checked every ten minutes, day and night. A creek reading is a measurement, not a safety clearance.
