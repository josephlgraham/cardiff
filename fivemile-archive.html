Where this comes from Every room reads the same file the live page reads, so nothing here is kept by hand and nothing can fall out of step. The photographs are this site's own and are credited to whoever made them. Weather is the site's own Ambient Weather station. The creek is the USGS gauge on Fivemile Creek near Republic, station 02457595. Stories are headlines and summaries from the outlets that published them, kept with a link back to the original. Dates are the same two lists the calendar reads. Nothing here is ever deleted.
