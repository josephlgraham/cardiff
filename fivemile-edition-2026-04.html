Monthly

April ran a little warmer than usual and came in wetter than usual, and Five Mile Creek rose over the high water line once.

This edition was put together on September 15, 2026, after the month was over, from the records as they stood. Its calls were worked out by the same rule as every other month, from what was on file before each month began.

April along the creek

Weather

Our station
🌡️Hottest89° on Apr 12
🌙Coldest36° on Apr 20
🌧️Rain6.90 in

The average of the highs and lows came to 66.1 degrees, 2.5 degrees warmer than normal for April.

Our station measured 6.90 inches of rain, against a normal of 5.08 at the Birmingham airport. The wettest day was April 29, with 2.45 inches.

At the Birmingham airport, whose record goes back to 1930, it was the second warmest April on the books.

The creek

Five Mile Creek at Republic
🌊Highest4.07 ft on Apr 29
🪨Lowest1.15 ft on Apr 28
📈Days at high water2

It reached the high water line, 3.5 feet at Republic, on two days, in one separate rise.

Its highest reading, 4.07 feet on April 29, was lower than the 7.14 feet the creek reaches in a typical April since 2008.

The high water line is the one the masthead uses. It is not a flood stage, because this gauge does not have one.

How the calls did

The calls for April

Temperature
🛰️NOAARight
📜FIVEMILENo call

Set against the same month in 1991 to 2020 at the Birmingham airport, April finished in the warm third for temperature and the middle third for rain.

NOAA's outlook, issued March 31, gave a 40 percent chance of a warmer than normal month. That was right.

FIVEMILE made no call for April, because the record had no clear lean after a warm March.

Since August 2014, NOAA's temperature outlook for these three towns has been right 62 times and missed 33 times, with no lean 44 times. FIVEMILE has not made a call yet since January 2026. A call made by guessing would be right about one time in three.

Dogwood winter

The old belief
🪶This yearHeld

Dogwood winter is the old name for the cold snap that comes when the dogwoods bloom, which in north Alabama is usually early to mid April.

It came this year: the airport got down to 41 degrees on April 20, 12 degrees under the normal low.

The old belief

Blackberry winter

A cool spell comes every May when the blackberries bloom.

Blackberry winter is the old name for the cool spell that comes when the blackberries bloom, usually early to mid May.

The airport record can test it. It counts as come when a night between May 1 and May 20 runs at least 10 degrees colder than the normal low for its date, after the year's first day of 85 degrees. In the 96 years on the record, it held in 75.

Where this comes from The month's weather is the FIVEMILE weather station. Normals, ranks, records and the thirds a month is scored in are the official National Weather Service record kept at the Birmingham airport since 1930, set against 1991 to 2020. The creek is the USGS gauge on Fivemile Creek near Republic. The outlook is the monthly outlook from NOAA's Climate Prediction Center, read at the middle of the three towns. The stories are the ones the news page ran, the sightings are iNaturalist's research grade records, and the dates are the calendar's. FIVEMILE's call is a rule written down in advance: it looks at how the month just gone finished and at what followed every earlier year that started the same way. Every sentence here was written ahead of time and filled in from those records, and no edition is changed after it runs.
