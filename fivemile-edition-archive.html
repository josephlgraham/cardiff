One edition a month: how the month went along the creek, how the forecasts for it did, and what the month after it held.

Each one is put together from the records once the month is over, and none is changed after it runs, so the calls in it are the calls it gets scored on.

Aug2026

The August 2026 Edition

August ran about as warm as usual and came in close to normal on rain, and Five Mile Creek never reached the high water line.

Where this comes from The month's weather is the FIVEMILE weather station. The records, the normals, and the thirds a month is scored in are the official National Weather Service record kept at the Birmingham airport. The creek is the USGS gauge near Republic, and the outlook is NOAA's Climate Prediction Center. Every sentence in an edition was written ahead of time and filled in from those records.
