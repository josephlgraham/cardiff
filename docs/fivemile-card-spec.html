Eleven card treatments, two badge systems, one size scale. Every class in this file is production ready. Copy the CSS block at the top of the source into fivemile-common.css without renaming anything, then lift the markup for each card from the examples below.

The size scale

Cards come in three heights and one row height. Everything on the site is one of these four. This is what makes a page look composed rather than assembled, and it is the rule most likely to erode, because every new card wants to be slightly taller than the one before it.

NeverMix two card heights in the same row. If a row needs one tall card and two short ones, the row is wrong, not the cards. Split it into two rows or move something.

Two badge systems

The homepage mixes content from every section, so a reader has to be able to tell at a glance which page a card came from and which town it concerns. Two questions, two systems, and they differ in shape and construction rather than only in hue, so they stay legible in black and white and to a colorblind reader.

The towns have no colors. An earlier draft gave each town its own, and it was a mistake: Brookside drew Cardiff red, the brand color, while Cardiff drew the weakest tan of the three. That is a hierarchy encoded in CSS on a site whose entire premise is that there is not one. All three badges are now identical, and the town name is the only thing distinguishing them, which is the correct amount of difference.

A useful side effect is that color is now free. Nothing competes with the section tags, so they can carry hue without ambiguity.

Never fill a section tag with its bar color, never put a border on a town badge, and never show a section tag on that section's own page, because the reader is already there and it is noise.

08 · Notice

.card-notice and .card-notice.info · height auto · full width

New, and the set needed it. There was nowhere to put a bacteria advisory, a road closure, or a correction. The announcement strip is one line and scrolls, so it cannot carry anything a reader has to actually absorb. Red for anything affecting safety or access, brown for everything else.

Water advisory

Give the creek a few dry days

Two inches of rain fell over the weekend. ADEM lists the lower seven miles of Five Mile Creek as impaired for E. coli, and bacteria rise after weather like this. Fishing is fine and the state puts no restriction on eating what you catch, but hold off on wading and swimming until midweek.

ADEM Fivemile Creek TMDL, June 2025

Correction

Council meets Thursday, not Tuesday

Friday's note gave the wrong night for the Graysville council. It meets on the first and third Thursday. Thanks to the reader who wrote in.

RedSafety, water quality, closures, anything a reader could be harmed by not knowing.
BrownCorrections, schedule changes, housekeeping.
RuleEvery red notice names its source. A warning with no provenance is a rumour.

11 · Plat frame

.card-plat · height auto · heritage page only

The surveyor's sheet, with a faint grid and red registration ticks at opposing corners. It deliberately breaks the 14px radius rule, which is why it is restricted to framing heritage sections and is never used for a data card or anywhere else.

The seam and the creek

Coal opened at Cardiff in 1880 and at Brookside in 1886, and Sloss ran the whole cluster out of Brookside because that is where the washer was. The rail line the Georgia Pacific laid along Five Mile Creek in the 1880s is the only reason any of these three towns exist where they do.

Rules that apply to all of them

One per purposeA creek reading looks identical on the homepage, the almanac, and the news page. If two pages show the same kind of thing, they use the same card.
No nestingNever put a card inside a card. If content inside a card needs structure, use rows, a hairline grid, or dotted leaders.
No shadowsBorders and fills only. A drop shadow is the fastest route back to looking like every other site.
Radius14px everywhere. The three exceptions are deliberate: the index tab squares its top left corner, the notice squares its left edge, and the plat frame uses 2px.
Colour disciplineTowns have no colour at all. Section colour appears only on tag left bars. Cardiff red belongs to the masthead, the department bar, the seed packet band, red notices, and nothing else.
Empty by defaultEvery card must look intentional with no image and no data. At launch most of them will have neither.
SentencesEvery blurb, caption, and note is a complete sentence. Kickers, labels, units, and the large numeral are not.
TouchAny tappable card is at least 44px in its smallest dimension. Body text never below 16px.
