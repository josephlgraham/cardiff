Where this comes from An Ambient Weather station in Cardiff, read every ten minutes and rolled up at the end of each day from its own five minute record. It reports for all three towns, because one rain gauge on this ground is closer to the truth here than a regional model is. Days before the station went up were imported from its own history export. Today is not in the table, because today is not over; it is on the almanac.
